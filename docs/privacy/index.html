Cardilyn Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026 · Contact: bantu.ryan@gmail.com

Overview

Cardilyn is a personal organizer for family health information (medications, vitals, visits, and clinician sharing). It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

What we store

  • Account details such as email and display name
  • Family membership and invites
  • Medications, medication logs, vitals, doctor contacts, appointments, clinical notes, and other information you enter
  • Health samples you choose to sync:
    • iOS — Apple Health / HealthKit (for example heart rate, steps, ECG summaries)
    • Android — Health Connect (for example heart rate, steps, blood pressure, weight, glucose)
  • Doctor verification details you submit (including profile photo, where provided)

Where it is stored

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase)
  • On-device Firestore cache for offline use
  • Local notification / reminder schedules on your device
  • Apple Health and Health Connect data remain on your device until you connect and sync into Cardilyn

How we use it

  • To provide your shared family health workspace
  • To schedule medication and visit reminders on your device
  • To generate health reports you choose to create or share
  • To support doctor registration and patient–doctor access you approve

Sharing

  • Family members you invite can view and edit shared family health data
  • Approved clinicians you connect may access records you authorize
  • When you use Share (for example Email or WhatsApp), you choose where a report goes
  • We do not sell your health data

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete logs inside the app
  • You can leave or be removed from a family
  • You can delete your account from Settings
  • You can disconnect Apple Health in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health
  • You can revoke Health Connect access in Health Connect settings on Android
